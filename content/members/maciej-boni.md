Maciej Boni is a Professor of Biology at Temple University's Institute for Genomics and Evolutionary Medicine (iGEM). His research focuses on drug-resistance management in malaria, as well as the epidemiology, seroepidemiology, and evolution of human influenza. His lab also works on detecting and reconstructing recombination histories.
