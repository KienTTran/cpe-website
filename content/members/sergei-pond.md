Dr. Sergei Pond is a Professor of Biology and the Associate Dean for Research and Innovation at Temple's College of Science and Technology. His research focuses on the development of statistical models and computational tools for comparative analysis of sequence data, particularly from evolving pathogens like HIV. He has been a professor at Temple University since 2016.
